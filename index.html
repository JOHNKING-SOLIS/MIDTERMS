Preprocessing workflow

01 · Load dataRead the supplied CSV into a dataframe.
02 · InspectReview shape, columns, data types, examples, and summary statistics.
03 · Missing valuesTrim text, standardize blanks, and transparently handle the missing gender label.
04 · DuplicatesCheck exact duplicate rows and remove duplicates if present.
05 · Data typesNormalize column names, convert age to numeric, and parse dates.
06 · OutliersUse the IQR rule to flag potential age outliers without automatically deleting records.
07 · Feature engineeringCreate incident month, year, decade, and season from the date.
08 · EncodingCreate a separate model-ready dataset with one-hot encoded low-cardinality categories.
09 · ScalingStandardize numeric age in the model-ready dataset when suitable.
10 · Save datasetsKeep a readable cleaned dataset and a separate model-ready table.
11 · ValidateCheck saved data shape, missing values, duplicates, and data types.
12 · VisualizeBuild ten charts from the cleaned data and interpret them cautiously.
